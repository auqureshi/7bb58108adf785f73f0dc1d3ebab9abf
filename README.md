# The Comeback 💪 Training for two

A 13-week coached program to get Ali and Aamna back in the gym: **lean, strong, and functional.**
Some sessions together, some solo, and all of them progressive and tracked.

## How it works

```
1. Tell the coach   →  "Going together tonight, ~60 min, legs a bit sore"
                       "Going alone tomorrow morning, 45 min"
2. Coach writes it  →  a session sheet in sessions/ and an updated app, with weights for each of you
3. You train        →  note your weights and reps (or just remember roughly)
4. Report back      →  tap "Build my report" in the app, copy, paste into the chat
                        (or just: "Ali goblet 24 kg × 10s, easy. Aamna 12 kg, felt good. Relay 9:12.")
5. Coach updates    →  progress log, PRs, streaks, next session gets harder
```

Messy reports are fine. A quick voice-to-text is plenty.

## Rules of the comeback

1. **Showing up beats being perfect.** And **never miss twice.**
2. **Leave reps in the tank for the first 3 weeks.** Patience now means PRs in January.
3. **Log everything.** It takes 30 seconds, and it's what makes the plan progressive.
4. **Compete with your last session, not with each other.**
5. **Protein at every meal, walk every day, sleep 7+ hours.** That's where "lean" happens.
6. **Soreness is fine, pain isn't.** Swap the exercise and tell the coach.

## The 13 weeks at a glance

| Weeks | Phase | What it feels like |
|---|---|---|
| 1–3 | **Re-Entry** | Easier than you want. Technique, baselines, rebuilding tolerance |
| 4–7 | **Build** | Weights go up every week. Barbells back. Intervals get spicy |
| 8 | **Deload** | New Year week. Half the volume, recharge |
| 9–12 | **Strength & Power** | Heavy 5 × 5s, jumps, throws, partner metcons |
| 13 | **Test & celebrate** | Benchmarks vs Week 2, photos, then design the next block |
