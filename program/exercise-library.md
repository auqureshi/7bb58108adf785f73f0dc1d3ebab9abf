# Exercise library

Key cues, plus an easier and a harder version of every move. Pick the version that lets you hit the target RPE with clean form.

## Squat
| Exercise | Cues | Easier | Harder |
|---|---|---|---|
| Goblet squat | Bell at chest, elbows inside knees, sit between your heels, chest proud | Box squat to a bench | Heels-elevated goblet · front squat |
| Back squat | Big belly breath, brace, bar on upper back, knees out, go only as deep as you control, drive the floor away | Goblet · Smith squat | Pause squat · tempo (3 s down) |
| Front squat | Elbows high, upright torso, sit straight down | Goblet | Pause front squat |
| Leg press | Feet mid-platform, lower until your hips start to tuck, don't lock out hard | Shorter range | Single-leg press |

## Hinge
| Exercise | Cues | Easier | Harder |
|---|---|---|---|
| DB / KB Romanian deadlift | Soft knees, hips back, weights slide down your legs, flat back, glutes finish the rep | Hip hinge with a dowel | Barbell RDL · single-leg RDL |
| Trap bar deadlift | Stand in the centre, chest up, push the floor away, stand tall | Raised handles | Low handles · conventional deadlift |
| Conventional deadlift | Bar over mid-foot, take the slack out of the bar, push the floor away, bar stays close | Trap bar · block pull | Deficit · pause at the knee |
| Hip thrust / glute bridge | Chin tucked, ribs down, drive through your heels, 1 s squeeze at the top | Floor bridge | Barbell hip thrust · single leg |
| KB swing | It's a hinge, not a squat. Snap the hips, the bell floats to chest height, arms are just ropes | KB deadlift | Heavier · single-arm |

## Single leg
| Exercise | Cues | Easier | Harder |
|---|---|---|---|
| Reverse lunge | Long step back, back knee kisses the floor, front heel drives you up | Hold a rack for balance | DBs · deficit |
| Bulgarian split squat | Rear foot on a bench, most of the weight on the front leg, slight forward lean | Split squat (foot on the floor) | DBs · front-foot deficit |
| Step-up | Whole foot on the box, drive through the heel, control the way down | Lower box | Higher box · DBs |
| Lateral lunge | Sit back into one hip, other leg straight, toes forward | Shorter range | Goblet-loaded |

## Push
| Exercise | Cues | Easier | Harder |
|---|---|---|---|
| Push-up | Hands under shoulders, body in one straight line, chest to fist height, elbows ~45° | Incline (hands on a bench or bar) | Deficit · tempo · weighted |
| DB bench press | Shoulder blades pinched and down, 2–3 s down, press up and slightly in | Floor press · machine press | Heavier · paused |
| Bench press | Grip just outside shoulder width, feet planted, touch the lower chest, drive up and back | DB bench | Paused · close grip |
| Half-kneeling 1-arm DB press | Squeeze the glute of the down knee, ribs down, press up and slightly forward | Seated, back supported | Standing · heavier |
| Overhead press | Squeeze glutes, ribs down, bar travels straight up, head through the window at the top | DB press · landmine press | Push press |
| Landmine press | Shoulder-friendly. Press up and forward and reach through at the top | Half-kneeling | Standing single-arm · split stance |

## Pull
| Exercise | Cues | Easier | Harder |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1-arm DB row | Flat back, elbow toward your hip pocket, 1 s pause, no twisting | Lighter · chest-supported | Heavier · Kroc row (higher reps) |
| Seated cable / chest-supported row | Chest tall, squeeze your shoulder blades, slow return | Machine row | Paused · single arm |
| Barbell row | Hinge to ~45°, pull to the belly button, no jerking | Chest-supported row | Pendlay row (from the floor) |
| Lat pulldown | Chest up, elbows down to your back pockets, bar to upper chest | Lighter · single-arm | Slow negatives |
| Pull-up progression | Shoulders down first, chest to the bar, full hang at the bottom | Assisted machine → band → 3–5 s negatives | Strict → weighted |
| Band pull-apart | Straight arms, pull the band to your chest, squeeze | Lighter band | Pause 2 s |

## Carry
| Exercise | Cues | Easier | Harder |
|---|---|---|---|
| Farmer carry | Tall, shoulders down, short quick steps, grip hard | Lighter · shorter distance | Heavier · longer · trap bar |
| Suitcase carry (one hand) | Don't lean. Stay perfectly level (anti-side-bend) | Lighter | Heavier |
| Front-rack / goblet carry | Elbows up, ribs down, breathe behind your brace | Lighter | Double KB front rack |

## Core
| Exercise | Cues | Easier | Harder |
|---|---|---|---|
| Dead bug | Lower back glued to the floor, slow opposite arm and leg, exhale as you extend | Legs only | Hold light weights |
| Pallof press | Stand side-on to the cable, press out and hold 2 s, don't let it rotate you | Lighter · wider stance | Half-kneeling · heavier |
| Plank / side plank | Squeeze glutes and quads, ribs down, one straight line | From the knees | Long-lever · feet elevated |
| Ab wheel | Ribs down, roll out only as far as you can keep your back flat | Kneeling, short range | Longer range · from the toes |
| Hanging knee / leg raise | Control the swing, tuck your pelvis at the top | Captain's chair | Straight legs · toes to bar |
| Copenhagen plank | Top leg on a bench, lift your hips, stay straight (inner thigh and groin durability) | Knee on the bench | Foot on the bench · longer hold |
| Landmine rotation | Rotate through your hips and feet, arms long, controlled | Lighter · half range | Heavier · faster |

## Power (always fresh, always fast, stop before you slow down)
| Exercise | Cues | Easier | Harder |
|---|---|---|---|
| Box jump | Swing your arms, land soft and quiet, **step down** (don't jump down) | Low box · squat jump | Higher box · seated box jump |
| Broad jump | Load the hips, jump out not up, stick the landing for 2 s | Shorter, focus on the landing | Consecutive jumps |
| Med-ball chest pass (to your partner) | Explosive push from the chest, catch soft | Lighter ball | Step into it · heavier |
| Med-ball slam | Reach tall, slam through the floor, hinge to pick it up | Lighter | Heavier · rotational slam |
| Rotational throw (wall or partner) | Power comes from the back hip, rotate and release | Lighter | Step-behind throw |

## Conditioning
| Tool | Notes |
|---|---|
| Rower | Legs → body → arms, then arms → body → legs. ~24–28 strokes/min for steady work. Don't just pull with your arms |
| Bike / air bike | Easiest on the joints. Great for intervals |
| Sled push | Arms long, low body angle, drive with your legs. Hard, joint-friendly, very "functional" |
| Incline walk | Zone 2 king: 10–12% incline, 4.5–5.5 km/h, no holding the rails |
| **Zone 2** | Conversational pace. You can talk in full sentences, nose-breathing is mostly possible. Builds your aerobic base and burns fat |
| **Intervals** | Hard (RPE 8–9) then easy, e.g. 30/30 or 20/40. Builds your engine fast |
