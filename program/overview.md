# The Comeback Program: a 13-week block for two

**Goal:** lean, strong, and functional for both of you.
**Structure:** 3 phases, plus a deload and a test week. Together sessions are built around partner formats. Solo sessions fill the gaps.

---

## Why it's built this way

- **Muscle memory is real.** Muscle you built before comes back much faster than it took to build the first time.
  Expect to be near your old numbers within roughly 4–8 consistent weeks.
- **Your tendons and joints don't come back as fast.** Neither does your work capacity. Tendons adapt more slowly than muscle.
  The classic comeback mistake is training like you never left. You get wrecked by soreness, miss a week, and the cycle repeats.
  **That's why the first 3 weeks are deliberately easier than you'll want them to be.**
- **Lean comes mostly from the other 23 hours:** protein, steps, sleep, and a modest calorie deficit. The gym builds the engine and the muscle.
  The kitchen reveals it. See [The other 23 hours](#the-other-23-hours).

## Weekly structure

**Targets:** Wife: 2–3 sessions/week. You: 3–4 sessions/week. **Minimum effective dose: 2 per week each.**
On non-gym days, walk. Aim for 8–10k steps; an evening walk together counts.

| Code | Session | Who | Focus |
|---|---|---|---|
| **T-A** | Together A: Squat & Push | Both | Squat, horizontal push/pull, single leg, core |
| **T-B** | Together B: Hinge & Pull | Both | Hinge, vertical push/pull, glutes, loaded carries |
| **T-C** | Together C: Athletic & Engine | Both | Power, single leg, push-ups, partner conditioning (the "lean" day) |
| **S-U** | Solo Upper: Strength & Arms | You (or wife) | Heavier pressing and pulling, shoulders, arms |
| **S-L** | Solo Lower: Athletic Legs & Engine | You (or wife) | Posterior chain, single leg, longer conditioning |

- **Together days** rotate **A → B → C → A …**
- **Solo days:** the coach picks S-U or S-L based on what you've done in the last 48 hours.
- **48-hour rule:** no heavy squat or heavy hinge on back-to-back days, and no hard upper session the day after another hard upper session.

## Partner formats (what makes together days work)

| Format | How it works | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| **You Go, I Go (YGIG)** | Share one station and alternate sets. Your partner's set is your rest. | Main strength lifts, shared machines |
| **Swap Stations** | Two exercises, one each, done at the same time. Then swap. One round = both have done both. | Supersets, saving time |
| **Synchro** | Same move, side by side, in rhythm. | Carries, lunges, burpees |
| **Relay** | One works while the other rests and cheers, then tag. | Rower, bike, sled finishers |
| **Hold & Work** | One works while the other holds a position (plank or wall sit) until they swap. | Brutal-but-fun finishers |

**Partner finisher menu** (the coach picks one to match the phase)
- **Comeback Relay:** rower or bike, 4–6 rounds of 250 m (or 45 s) each, alternating.
- **Plank Swings:** 50–100 KB swings shared between you. Whoever isn't swinging holds a plank. Swap whenever you like.
- **Synchro Burpees:** 5 × 10 burpees in sync, 30 s rest.
- **Bike 30/30:** 30 s hard while partner rests, tag, 8–12 rounds each.
- **Carry Ladder:** farmer carry 20 m, 40 m, 60 m, walked side by side (Synchro). Rest 45 s between rungs; go heavier each time through.
- **Partner Chipper (Phase 3):** 100 cal row · 80 swings · 60 wall balls · 40 lunges · 20 burpees, split however you like. One works at a time.

## Phase prescriptions

### Phase 1: Re-Entry (Weeks 1–3)
- **Main lifts:** 3 × 8–10. W1 @ RPE 6 → W2 @ RPE 7 → W3 @ RPE 7–8
- **Accessories:** 2–3 × 10–15 @ RPE 7
- **Finishers:** 6–8 min, moderate. RPE 7 at most
- **Rest:** 60–90 s (partner formats handle this)
- **Rules:** no failure, no maxing, leave your ego in the locker. Mostly dumbbells, kettlebells, and machines. Barbells return in Week 3 if you're moving well.

### Phase 2: Build (Weeks 4–7)
- **Main lifts:** 4 × 6–8 @ RPE 7–8, double progression
- **Secondary:** 3 × 8–12 · **Accessories:** 3 × 10–15 (last set can reach RPE 9)
- **Power:** 3 × 3 jumps or throws before the main lift
- **Finishers:** 10–12 min intervals

### Week 8: Deload
Same exercises at roughly half the sets and ~90% of the loads. Finishers easy (RPE 6). Walk, stretch, sleep.
This isn't lost time. It's where the adaptation shows up.

### Phase 3: Strength & Power (Weeks 9–12)
- **Main lifts:** 5 × 5 (W9–10) → 5 × 3–5 heavier (W11–12) @ RPE 8
- **Power:** 4 × 3–5, fast and crisp, always fresh (first thing after the warm-up)
- **Accessories:** 3 × 8–12
- **Finishers:** 12–15 min partner metcons

### Week 13: Test & celebrate
Benchmarks, optional rep-max tests (e.g. a 5RM), photos and measurements. Compare against Week 2. Then we design the next block from the results.

## Standard warm-up (8–10 min)

1. **Raise (4–5 min):** easy bike, rower, or incline walk. You should be able to talk.
2. **Mobilise and activate (1 round):** world's greatest stretch × 3/side · cat-cow × 6 · glute bridge × 10 ·
   bodyweight squat × 8 (pause at the bottom) · inchworm × 4 · band pull-apart × 15 · dead bug × 5/side
3. **Ramp the first lift:** 2–3 lighter sets building up to your working weight (e.g. 50% × 8, 75% × 5).

## Progression rules

**RPE (how hard a set was)**

| RPE | Reps left in the tank | Feels like |
|---|---|---|
| 10 | 0 | Absolute max |
| 9 | 1 | Maybe one more |
| 8 | 2 | Hard, two clean reps left |
| 7 | 3 | Challenging but crisp |
| 6 | 4 | Moderate, bar moves fast |
| ≤5 | 5+ | Warm-up |

1. **Double progression.** Every exercise has a rep range (e.g. 3 × 8–10). Keep the same weight until you hit the **top** of the range on
   **all** sets at or below the target RPE. Next session, add load and start again at the bottom of the range.
2. **Load jumps.** Upper-body barbell +2.5 kg · lower-body barbell +5 kg (+2.5 kg once it's heavy) · dumbbells: next pair up · machines: next pin.
3. **Bad-day protocol** (under 6 hours' sleep, stressed, run-down, or very sore): same session, one set fewer per exercise, about 10% lighter,
   and an easy finisher or none. **A bad-day session still counts as a full win.**
4. **Missed time.** Up to 1 week: repeat the last week. 2–3 weeks: loads down 10–15%, rebuild over one week.
   4+ weeks: restart the current phase from its first week.
5. **Stalls.** If the same weight gives no rep progress for 3 sessions, drop it 10% and build back, or swap the variation. The coach will flag this.
6. **Pain rule.** Muscle soreness is fine. Sharp pain, joint pain, or pain that changes how you move means stop that exercise and swap it
   for a pain-free one, then tell the coach. If it lasts more than a week, see a physio.

## The other 23 hours

| Lever | Target | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Protein** | 1.6–2.2 g per kg of bodyweight per day, about 30–40 g per meal | Builds and keeps muscle while you lean out. Keeps you full |
| **Calories** | Modest deficit (~300–500 kcal/day) if fat loss is the goal. Aim to lose ~0.5% of bodyweight per week | Crash diets kill strength, mood, and consistency |
| **Steps** | 8–10k per day | The most underrated fat-loss tool. Walk together |
| **Sleep** | 7–9 h | Recovery, appetite control, and strength all depend on it |
| **Creatine** (optional) | 3–5 g monohydrate daily | Well-researched for strength and muscle in men and women. Not essential |
| **Tracking** | Weekly *average* weight · waist every 2 weeks · photos every 4 weeks | Daily scale weight is noise. Trends are signal |
