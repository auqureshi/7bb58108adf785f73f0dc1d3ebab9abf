## Training style: what Ali knows and enjoys

- **Body-part split, bodybuilding style:** chest/triceps, back/biceps, shoulders, legs, plus "pump" arm supersets.
- **Cross-functional circuits:** 5–6 stations, 3 rounds, 15 reps (row, bench, lunges, curls, plank, shuttle runs).
- **Boxing finishers, regularly:** straight punches and hooks for 60 s, or 20 s jab / uppercut / hook with 10 s rest, × 3 sets.
- **Favourite exercise: "bar left right".** This is the **landmine rotation**: a barbell anchored in a corner or landmine, swung side to side.
  Done as a drop set at 30 → 20 → 10 kg in Dec 2022.
- **Running:** 400 m repeats at 12–13 km/h, and shuttle ladders (10-20-30-40).

**How the plan uses this:**
- Boxing is now on the partner finisher menu ("Predrag Rounds").
- "Bar left right" comes into the plan early: light from Phase 1 on Ali's solo upper day, and in T-C.
- Solo upper days (S-U) keep Ali's arm pump supersets: curl + hammer, pushdown + overhead extension.
- Circuits match the T-C partner metcon format, so they'll feel familiar.

## Predrag's cues worth keeping

These are shown in the app on the matching exercises as "Predrag's cue".

- **Goblet squat:** "Go to 90°, pause a second at the bottom, and don't lock your legs when you stand."
- **Smith squat:** "Knees out."
- **Trap bar:** "Arms long, shoulders back, then lift."
- **Lat pulldown:** "Hold it down for a second."
- **Seated row:** "Don't rock forward and back. Stay straight, slightly leaning back."
- **Lateral raise:** "Elbows up."
- **Hinge to the floor (RDL):** "Feet close, back straight, push your hips back, then bend your knees until the weights touch the ground."
- **Bulgarian split squat:** "Back foot on the bench. The back knee needs to touch the ground."
- **Curls:** "Twist as much as you can."
