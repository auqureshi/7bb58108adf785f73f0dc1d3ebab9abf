## Coach's brief

Today isn't about how much you lift. Success today is walking out feeling like you could do it all again tomorrow,
and still being able to walk on Monday.

Today you have four jobs:
1. **Show up.** ✅ Already done by reading this.
2. **Move well.** Smooth reps, full range, controlled lowering.
3. **Find your starting weights.**
4. **Write them down.** They're your baselines, and the starting point for every session after this.

Your old strength is still in there, and it comes back fast. Your tendons need 2–3 weeks to catch up with your muscles,
so we're giving them that time. Patience now means PRs in January.

### How to find your weight today (calibration)
Start conservative. After each set, ask yourself: **"How many more good reps could I have done?"**

| Answer | Do this |
|---|---|
| 5 or more | Go up one step (next dumbbell, next pin) |
| **3–4** | **Perfect. Stay here.** This is RPE 6–7 |
| 0–2 | Too heavy. Go down a step |

The weight that lands on "3–4 left" is your number. Log it.

## Warm-up, together (10 min)

1. **5 min easy cardio side by side:** bike, rower, or incline walk, at a pace where you can chat.
2. **Movement prep, 1 round, follow each other:**
   - World's greatest stretch × 3/side
   - Cat-cow × 6
   - Glute bridge × 10 (squeeze 2 s at the top)
   - Bodyweight squat × 8 (3 s down, 2 s pause at the bottom)
   - Inchworm × 4 (add a push-up if it feels good)
   - Band pull-apart × 15 (no band: arm circles × 10 each way)
   - Dead bug × 5/side (lower back pressed into the floor)

## Block A: Swap Stations · 3 rounds (~12 min)

One of you does A1 while the other does A2, then swap. Once you've both done both, that's one round.
Rest about 60 s and go again. **Before your first squat set, do one light feeler set of 8.**

| | Exercise | Sets × reps | Effort | Cues |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **A1** | **Goblet squat** (DB or KB) | 3 × 10 | RPE 6 | Bell held at your chest, elbows inside your knees. Sit down *between* your heels with your chest proud. 3 s down, drive up. |
| **A2** | **1-arm DB row** (hand and knee on a bench) | 3 × 10/side | RPE 6 | Flat back. Pull your elbow toward your hip pocket, pause 1 s at the top, no twisting. |

## Block B: Swap Stations · 3 rounds (~12 min)

| | Exercise | Sets × reps | Effort | Cues |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **B1** | **DB Romanian deadlift** | 3 × 10 | RPE 6 | Soft knees. Push your hips back like you're shutting a car door with your butt. DBs slide down your thighs to mid-shin, back stays flat. Squeeze your glutes to stand tall. |
| **B2** | **DB bench press** (or push-ups) | 3 × 10 | RPE 6 | Shoulder blades pinched and down, feet planted. Lower for 2–3 s to chest level, then press up and slightly in. *Push-up option:* hands on a bench or Smith bar. Higher is easier. |

## Block C: Functional trio · 2 rounds (~10 min)

Do these side by side. Take turns on the pulldown (You Go, I Go).

| | Exercise | Sets × reps | Effort | Cues |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **C1** | **Lat pulldown** | 2 × 12 | RPE 6–7 | Chest up. Pull the bar to your upper chest, driving elbows down toward your back pockets. 2 s back up. |
| **C2** | **Reverse lunge** (bodyweight) | 2 × 6/side | Easy | Take a long step back, back knee kisses the floor softly, front heel drives you up. Hold a rack for balance if you need to. |
| **C3** | **Farmer carry** (Synchro) | 2 × 30–40 m | Moderate | Heavy-ish DBs or KBs. Walk tall with shoulders down and short, quick steps, side by side. |

---

## Finisher: "Comeback Relay" (~8 min)

**Rower** (or bike / ski erg). **4 rounds each.** You row **250 m**, then your partner rows 250 m, and so on.
Whoever is resting gets their breath back and cheers.
(Bike option: 45 s instead of 250 m.)

- **Pace: strong but controlled, RPE 7.** Not a sprint. This is not the day to feel sick.
- **Score:** total time for all 8 legs. **Log it. We're beating it in a few weeks.**

## What to expect over the next 48 hours

- **Soreness (DOMS) usually peaks 24–48 hours after training,** so Sunday into Monday. It's normal and a sign you're back.
- **The best fix is movement.** Go for a 30–45 minute walk together on Sunday, eat protein at every meal, and get a full night's sleep.
- **Don't skip the next session because you're sore.** Training sore at RPE 6 makes the soreness go away *faster*.
