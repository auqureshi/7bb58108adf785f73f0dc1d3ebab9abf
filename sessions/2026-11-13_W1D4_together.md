| | Exercise | Sets × reps | Ali | Aamna | Cues |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| B1 | **Reverse lunge with DBs** | 3 × 8/side · RPE 6 | Start ~8 kg each (Sat: bodyweight, easy · Predrag days: 8–10 kg) | Round 1 bodyweight, then light DBs if easy | Long step back, knee kisses the floor, drive through the front heel |

## Block C: Swap Stations · 3 rounds (~12 min) · rest 60 s

| | Exercise | Sets × reps | Ali | Aamna | Cues |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| C1 | **KB swing** | 3 × 12 · RPE 6 | Start 16 kg | Light bell (8–12 kg) | **First set: 8 KB deadlifts to learn the hinge**, then swing. Hips back (not a squat), snap the hips, arms are ropes, bell floats to chest height |
| C2 | **Landmine rotation ("bar left right")** | 3 × 8/side · RPE 6 | Sunday's weight, +2.5–5 kg if it was easy | Bar only or Sunday's weight | Arms long, pivot your back foot, turn from your hips |

**Partner coaching on the swings:** the one resting watches. If the swing looks like a squat, or the lower back works harder than the glutes, go back to deadlifts.

## Finisher: "Tag Team Relay" (~10 min) · RPE 7

**One round =** 8 KB swings + 6 push-ups (today's version) + 20 m suitcase carry with your bell (switch hands halfway).
Do a round, then tag your partner: **5 rounds each, 10 legs.** The app stopwatch tracks whose turn it is and times each leg.
**Score = total time.** We'll beat it in a few weeks. If the swings feel unsure, do 8 KB deadlifts instead.

## Cool-down (5 min)

Hip-flexor stretch 45 s/side · child's pose 45 s · doorway chest stretch 30 s/side · 5 slow breaths. **That wraps up Week 1.**
