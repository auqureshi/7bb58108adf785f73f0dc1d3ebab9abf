## Coach's brief

Ali asked for **shoulders with Aamna, no legs**, and what to combine with them. The choice is **back**, plus a short arm pump:

- **Shoulders + back (chosen):** pressing and pulling balance each other, which keeps shoulders healthy and builds the V-shape. Predrag ran "back & shoulder" days the same way.
- Shoulders + chest + triceps: all pushing. The front of the shoulder gets hit twice, with nothing pulling to balance it.
- Shoulders + arms only: a fine pump, but fewer big muscles working, so less calorie burn.

Machines and cables throughout, per their preference. The finisher is boxing, which is upper-body cardio with no leg work.

**Effort:** Week 2 is RPE 7, about 3 reps left. If this is the first session since Fri 13 Nov, stay at RPE 6 (about 4 left), following the missed-time rule.
**Loads:** still no logged numbers, so start from Machine Day (13 Nov) weights, Predrag numbers for Ali, and the calibration rule.

## Finisher: "Predrag Rounds" (~8 min) · RPE 7

Shadow-box side by side: **20 s jab–cross, 20 s uppercuts, 20 s hooks, 10 s rest, × 3 = 1 set. 2 sets, 60 s apart.** If your shoulders are cooked, do 1 set.
