# Week 1 · Session 2: Together · "Round Two" 🥊

**Sun 8 Nov 2026** · App: https://claude.ai/artifact/Ba3fQQnaSsQJhDLfW1WFiu · Template **T-B** (Pull, Press & Glutes, adapted for a back-to-back day) · Phase 1: Re-Entry · ~55 min · **Effort: RPE 6**

---

## Coach's brief

You're training two days in a row. Session 1 hit legs and hinge (goblet squats, RDLs, lunges, carries), so today shifts the load:
**pulling up, pressing overhead, glutes, core, and a boxing finish.** The legs only get light, flushing work.

Why not the standard T-B? Its main lift is the DB RDL, and repeating that within 24 hours of yesterday's RDLs would pile onto the hamstring soreness.
So the hinge becomes **bodyweight back extensions** (glutes and lower back, light on the hamstrings), the pulldown becomes your **pull-up baseline**, and the farmer carry becomes a **suitcase carry**.

**Soreness check:** if your legs are 7/10 sore or worse, or a joint hurts, use Plan B.

Jobs today: **loosen up the soreness · set your pull-up baseline · learn "bar left right" · box it out together.**

## Finisher: "Predrag Rounds" (~8 min) · RPE 7

Shadow-boxing side by side, same punches at the same time. The app has a round timer that beeps at every switch:
two high beeps mean punch, one low beep means rest.

- **One round:** 20 s jab–cross → 20 s uppercuts → 20 s hooks → 10 s rest
- **One set:** 3 rounds. **Do 2 sets**, with 60 s rest between them
- Fast hands, light feet, breathe out on every punch. No space? Use the same timing on the bike.

## Cool-down (5 min)

Child's pose 45 s (reach to each side) · half-kneeling hip-flexor stretch 45 s/side · doorway chest stretch 30 s/side · 5 slow breaths lying down.

## Plan B (very sore or 35 min)

5-min bike → Block A × 2 (back extensions with a smaller range) → Block B × 2 → 1 set of Predrag Rounds. Skip Block C.
