## Coach's brief

Aamna's first solo session. Everything is easy to set up alone: dumbbells, one cable machine, a bench and the rower.
She trained Saturday and Sunday, so this makes **3 sessions and completes her Week 1 target**.

Why this mix: Sat was legs and hinge and Sun was pulling, pressing and core, with Monday as rest. Tuesday is 72 h after the leg day,
so squats and step-ups are fine again. Full-body is the best use of a solo hour.
Kettlebell swings from the T-C template are left out on purpose: learn them with a partner first.

**No logged numbers yet** (Session 1 and 2 reports still to come), so loads use the calibration rule.
For the goblet squat and carry, start from Saturday's weights.

## Warm-up (8 min)

5 min incline walk or easy bike → world's greatest stretch × 3/side · glute bridge × 12 · band pull-apart × 15 · bodyweight squat × 8 (slow) · inchworm × 4

## Block A: Superset · 3 rounds (~12 min) · rest 60 s

| | Exercise | Sets × reps | Aamna | Cues |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A1 | **Goblet squat** | 3 × 10 · RPE 6 | Saturday's weight. If it felt easy, one dumbbell up | Bell at chest, elbows inside knees, 3 s down |
| A2 | **Seated cable row** | 3 × 12 · RPE 6 | A weight she could pull ~15 times | Chest tall, pull to belly button, no rocking |

## Block B: Superset · 3 rounds (~12 min) · rest 60 s

| | Exercise | Sets × reps | Aamna | Cues |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| B1 | **DB step-up** | 3 × 8/side · RPE 6 | Round 1 bodyweight on a knee-height box. Add light DBs only if easy | Whole foot on the box, drive through the heel, 2 s down |
| B2 | **Push-up** (pick a height) | 3 × 8–10 · RPE 6 | Bench / Smith bar / knees / floor. Choose the height that leaves 3–4 reps in the tank | Body in one line, chest to a fist from the surface |

## Block C: Core & carry · 2 rounds (~8 min) · rest 45 s

| | Exercise | Sets × reps | Aamna | Cues |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| C1 | **Pallof press** | 2 × 10/side | Light: the handle stays still for 2 s | Side-on, press out, don't let it twist you |
| C2 | **Farmer carry** | 2 × 30–40 m | Saturday's carry weight, or one step up | Tall, shoulders down, quick steps |

## Finisher: "Row Strong" (~6 min) · RPE 7

6 rounds: **30 s strong row / 30 s easy paddle.** The app timer beeps at each switch. No rower? Use the bike with the same timing.

## Cool-down (5 min)

Hip-flexor stretch 45 s/side · child's pose 45 s · doorway chest stretch 30 s/side · 5 slow breaths.
